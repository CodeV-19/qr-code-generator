About This QR Code Generator

This free tool creates QR codes instantly in your browser — no account, no watermark, and no data ever leaves your device. Enter a website link, plain text, Wi-Fi details, or an email address, and export a scannable QR code as a PNG image or a scalable SVG in seconds.

How to use it

1. Choose a QR type above (URL, text, Wi-Fi, or email). 2. Fill in the details. 3. Pick PNG or SVG as your file format — SVG is ideal for print and large-format use since it scales without losing quality. 4. (Optional) Upload a logo to embed in the center of the code — it's automatically sized and padded so the QR code stays scannable. 5. Click "Generate QR Code." 6. Click "Share" to send it directly, or "Download" to save the file and use it anywhere — print, packaging, business cards, posters, or menus.
